Definition. The strict downset sieve of a direct category

2026-07-19 · Steven Schaefer · category-theory presheaf ideal subobject

Let 𝒞︀ carry a direct structure. The strict downset 𝖲𝗍𝗋𝗂𝖼𝗍𝖣𝗈𝗐𝗇(𝑥) of an object 𝑥 is the presheaf of morphisms into 𝑥 from strictly lower objects:

𝖲𝗍𝗋𝗂𝖼𝗍𝖣𝗈𝗐𝗇(𝑥)(𝑦)={𝑓:𝑦→𝑥∣𝑦≺𝑥},

with restriction by precomposition — well defined since degrees are non-decreasing, so precomposing can only stay strictly below.

The evident inclusion 𝖲𝗍𝗋𝗂𝖼𝗍𝖣𝗈𝗐𝗇(𝑥)↣よ𝑥 makes 𝖲𝗍𝗋𝗂𝖼𝗍𝖣𝗈𝗐𝗇(𝑥) a sieve on 𝑥. It is moreover a proper sieve, as it exlcudes the identity.
