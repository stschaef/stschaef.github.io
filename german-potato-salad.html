Recipe. German Potato Salad

2025-02-19 · recipe
Cook 1 h 5 min Cuisine German

Ingredients
  • 5 pounds potatoes
  • 1 pound bacon
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 2 cups mayo
  • 1 cup cold water
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 onion

Method
  1. Peel and cube potatoes.
  2. Boil potatoes until fork tender, but still slightly firm.
  3. Cook bacon, then crumble. Reserve bacon grease.
  4. Dice onion and cook in bacon grease until brown.
  5. Mix bacon, onion, potatoes, mayo, sour cream, cold water, 1 tbsp salt, 1 tbsp pepper, and sugar in an oven-safe bowl.
  6. Bake for 60 minutes at 350 Fahrenheit. Broil for 5-7 minutes, or until golden brown.

Notes

This is another cherished recipe from my mother.

As a child, I was extraordinarily averse to mayo, sour cream, cottage cheese, etc. I didn’t understand how delicious this recipe was until I was about 20 or so. I can’t believe I had missed out for so long.
