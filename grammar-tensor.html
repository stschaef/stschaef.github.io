Definition. Tensor of Grammars

parsing lambekd intrinsically-correct

For formal grammars 𝐴 and 𝐵, their tensor 𝐴⊗𝐵 represents the concatenation of the languages they describe. A parse for 𝐴⊗𝐵 at a string 𝑤 consists of a splitting of 𝑤 into a prefix 𝑢 and suffix 𝑣, along with a parse of 𝑢 in 𝐴 and a parse of 𝑣 in 𝐵.

Formally, it is defined as:

(𝐴⊗𝐵)(𝑤)=∑𝑤=𝑢𝑣𝐴(𝑢)×𝐵(𝑣)

This operation is exactly the Day convolution of 𝐴 and 𝐵, where we view formal grammars as presheaves over the monoid of strings.
