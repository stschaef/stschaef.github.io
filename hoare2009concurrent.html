Kleene Algebra with Commutativity Conditions Is Undecidable azevedodeamorim-2025-kleeneCSL · 2025
We prove that the equational theory of Kleene algebra with commutativity conditions on primitives (or atomic terms) is undecidable, thereby settling a longstanding open question in the theory of Kleene algebra. While this question has also been recently solved independently by Kuznetsov, our results hold even for weaker theories that do not support the induction axioms of Kleene algebra.

Kleene algebra with tests kozen1997kleeneTOPLAS · 1997
We introduce Kleene algebra with tests, an equational system for manipulating programs. We give a purely equational proof, using Kleene algebra with tests and commutativity conditions, of the following classical result: every while program can be simulated by a while program with at most one while loop. The proof illustrates the use of Kleene algebra with tests and commutativity conditions in program equivalence proofs.

A Completeness Theorem for Kleene Algebras and the Algebra of Regular Events KOZEN1994366I&C · 1994
We give a finitary axiomatization of the algebra of regular events involving only equations and equational implications. Unlike Salomaa′s axiomatizations, the axiomatization given here is sound for all interpretations over Kleene algebras.
