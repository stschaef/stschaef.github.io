Coalgebras as a displayed category

2026-07-19 · Steven Schaefer · displayed-category-theory category-theory F-algebra

Fix an endofunctor 𝐹:𝒞︀→𝒞︀. Coalgebras require no new construction: a coalgebra is an algebra in the opposite category. Define

CoalgStr(𝐹)=AlgStr(𝐹op),

a displayed category over 𝒞︀op, where 𝐹op:𝒞︀op→𝒞︀op is 𝐹 acting on the opposite category.

Concretely, over an object 𝑥 a displayed object is a structure map

𝛾∈𝒞︀(𝑥,𝐹𝑥).

The category of coalgebras is the opposite of the total category:

Coalg(𝐹)=(∫CoalgStr(𝐹))op.

The outer opposite returns morphisms to the direction of 𝒞︀: a morphism (𝑥,𝛾)→(𝑦,𝛿) is a map 𝑓:𝑥→𝑦 with

𝑓⋆𝛿=𝛾⋆𝐹𝑓.
