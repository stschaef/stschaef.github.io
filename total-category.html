Definition. Total category

2026-07-19 · Steven Schaefer · displayed-category-theory

The total category of a displayed category over 𝒞︀ collects the displayed data into a single category. Its objects are pairs of an object 𝑥 of 𝒞︀ with an object over it, and its morphisms are pairs of a morphism 𝑓:𝑥→𝑦 with a displayed morphism over 𝑓.

Projecting out the first components is a functor . Constructions presented displayed — algebras, Eilenberg–Moore categories — get their forgetful functor for free as this projection.
