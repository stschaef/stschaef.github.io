Theorem. Maximality of the strict downset among proper sieves

2026-07-19 · Steven Schaefer · category-theory presheaf ideal subobject

Call a direct structure reflecting when every morphism between objects of equal degree is a split epimorphism. In a reflecting direct category, every non-invertible-in-degree morphism strictly raises degree, and the strict downset is as large as a proper sieve can be:

If the direct structure is reflecting, then every proper sieve 𝑆 on 𝑥 refines into the strict downset:

𝑆⊆𝖲𝗍𝗋𝗂𝖼𝗍𝖣𝗈𝗐𝗇(𝑥).

For suppose 𝑆∋𝑓 with 𝑓:𝑦→𝑥 of equal degree. By reflection 𝑓 has a section 𝑠, and closure under precomposition gives 𝑆∋𝑠⋆𝑓=𝗂𝖽𝑥, contradicting properness. So every member of 𝑆 strictly raises degree, which is membership in 𝖲𝗍𝗋𝗂𝖼𝗍𝖣𝗈𝗐𝗇(𝑥).
