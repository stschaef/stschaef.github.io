Definition. Constant Presheaf

2025-02-12 · nLab · category-theory presheaf

The constant presheaf of a set 𝑋 on a category 𝐶 is a functor Δ(𝑋):𝐶𝑜𝑝→𝐒𝐞𝐭 such that

Δ(𝑋)𝑐≔𝑋

I often call this the discrete presheaf for 𝑋, but I don’t know if that’s standard.
