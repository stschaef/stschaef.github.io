Definition. Category of Elements

nLab · displayed-category-theory category-theory presheaf

Let 𝑃 be a presheaf on a category 𝒞︀. The category of elements of 𝑃 is the displayed category over 𝒞︀ whose displayed objects over 𝑐 are the elements 𝑝∈𝑃𝑐, and whose displayed morphisms over 𝑓:𝑐→𝑑 from 𝑝 to 𝑞 are proofs that (𝑃𝑓)(𝑞)=𝑝.

Since 𝑃𝑐 is a set, there is at most one displayed morphism over each 𝑓 between given elements: a morphism of elements is a morphism of 𝒞︀ that happens to carry 𝑞 back to 𝑝. Its total category is the classical category of elements ∫𝑃, and a universal element of 𝑃 is exactly a terminal object of ∫𝑃.
