Definition. Hylomorphism

category-theory F-algebra F-coalgebra

Fix an endofunctor 𝐹:𝒞︀→𝒞︀, a coalgebra 𝛾:𝑋→𝐹𝑋 and an algebra 𝛼:𝐹𝐵→𝐵. A hylomorphism (or coalgebra-to-algebra morphism) from 𝛾 to 𝛼 is a morphism ℎ:𝑋→𝐵 satisfying

ℎ=𝛾⋆𝐹ℎ⋆𝛼.
