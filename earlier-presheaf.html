Definition. Earlier on presheaves

2026-07-19 · Steven Schaefer · category-theory presheaf guarded-recursion well-founded left-adjoint

Later takes a limit over smaller indices. Dually, the earlier modality takes a colimit over larger indices: an element of ⊲𝑃 at 𝑥 is a 𝑃-element sitting at some object 𝑦 strictly above 𝑥, carried down along a chosen morphism 𝑥→𝑦.

Earlier is left adjoint to later:
