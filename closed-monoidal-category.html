Definition. Closed Monoidal Structure

2024-08-06 · category-theory monoidal-category

A monoidal category is left closed if for each 𝑌∈𝐶, the functor −⊗𝑌:𝐶→𝐶 has a right adjoint 𝑌⊸−:𝐶→𝐶 forming the left internal-hom out of 𝑌.

That is, for all 𝑋,𝑌,𝑍, there is a natural isomorphism 𝐶[𝑋⊗𝑌,𝑍]≅𝐶[𝑋,[𝑌,𝑍]]

There is an obvious right-handed variant 𝑌⟜− that is right adjoint to 𝑌⊗−.

If 𝐶 is both right and left closed, the monoidal category is simply called closed (or perhaps biclosed).
