Theorem. Beck–Chevalley

category-theory adjunction families

Let 𝐶 be a category with pullbacks and consider a pullback square

The change of base adjoints commute with reindexing along it: the canonical comparison maps

∑𝑓′𝑔∗→ℎ∗∑𝑓ℎ∗∏𝑓→∏𝑓′𝑔∗

are isomorphisms, the second whenever 𝐶 is locally cartesian closed. This is the Beck–Chevalley condition, part of what it means for a fibration to have (co)products ([1], Definition 1.9.4). For the codomain fibration ∑𝑓 is postcomposition with 𝑓, and the condition for ∑ is the pasting lemma for pullbacks ([1], Proposition 1.9.8). When every 𝑢∗ has both adjoints, the condition holds for ∑ if and only if it holds for ∏ ([1], Lemma 1.9.7).

For families. In families of types, take 𝑋′=∑(𝑥,𝑦′):𝑋×𝑌′(𝑓𝑥=ℎ𝑦′) with 𝑔 and 𝑓′ the projections. For 𝐴:𝑋→𝐓𝐲𝐩𝐞,

(ℎ∗∑𝑓𝐴)𝑦′=∑𝑥:𝑋(𝑓𝑥=ℎ𝑦′)×𝐴𝑥
(∑𝑓′𝑔∗𝐴)𝑦′=∑(𝑥,𝑦″,𝑝):𝑋′(𝑦″=𝑦′)×𝐴𝑥

and the second contracts to the first, since ∑𝑦″:𝑌′(𝑦″=𝑦′) is contractible. The same contraction under a ∏ gives (ℎ∗∏𝑓𝐴)𝑦′≅(∏𝑓′𝑔∗𝐴)𝑦′.

Along a product projection 𝜋1:𝑌×𝑍→𝑌 the adjoints ∑𝜋1 and ∏𝜋1 are the existential and universal quantifiers, and the squares with sides 𝑢×𝗂𝖽 and 𝑢 are pullbacks, so Beck–Chevalley for them says that quantification commutes with substitution in the free variables ([1], after Definition 1.9.4).
