Steven Schaefer stevenschaeferPhD · University of Michigan
PhD advisorMax S. New
PhDUniversity of Michigan
UndergraduateUniversity of Michigan

I study the design and implementation of intrinsically correct software. In a sufficiently rich type system, a program’s type signature alone can carry a proof that it behaves as specified.

I am especially interested in verified compiler frontends, where parsing and typechecking algorithms can be built correct-by-construction.

Outside of research, I like to bake, garden, climb, play chess and play Tetris. Please send me a challenge in either (or anything else for that matter).
