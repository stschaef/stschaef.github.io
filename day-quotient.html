Definition. The Quotient and its Right Adjoint in Day Convolution

2026-10-01 · parsing lambekd category-theory

Let 𝐶 be a small monoidal category. Recall that for presheaves 𝐴,𝐵:𝐶op→𝐒𝐞𝐭, the Day convolution provides a closed monoidal structure:

(𝐴⊗𝐵)(𝑐)=∫𝑢,𝑣𝐶[𝑐,𝑢⊗𝑣]×𝐴(𝑢)×𝐵(𝑣)
(𝐴⊸𝐵)(𝑐)=∫𝑢𝐴(𝑢)→𝐵(𝑢⊗𝑐)

which forms an adjunction 𝐴⊗−⊣𝐴⊸−.

For covariant functors 𝐴:𝐶→𝐒𝐞𝐭 and presheaves 𝐵:𝐶op→𝐒𝐞𝐭, we can define the quotient and its right adjoint, each of which is a presheaf on 𝐶:

(𝐷𝐴𝐵)(𝑐)=∫𝑢𝐴(𝑢)×𝐵(𝑢⊗𝑐)
(𝐵𝐴)(𝑐)=∫𝑢,𝑣𝐶[𝑢⊗𝑣,𝑐]→(𝐴(𝑢)→𝐵(𝑣))

These form the adjunction 𝐷𝐴⊣−𝐴.
