Definition. The Schanuel Topos

2026-10-01 · nLab · category-theory nominal-set

Let 𝕀 be the category of finite sets and injections. The Schanuel topos is the category of pullback-preserving functors 𝕀op→𝐒𝐞𝐭.

Equivalently, it is the category of nominal sets, which are sets equipped with an action by the group of permutations on a countable set of names 𝔸, such that every element has finite support.
