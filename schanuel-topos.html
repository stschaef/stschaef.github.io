Definition. The Schanuel Topos

category-theory

Let 𝕀 be the category of finite sets and injections. The Schanuel topos is the category of presheaves on 𝕀op (i.e. covariant functors 𝕀→𝐒𝐞𝐭) that satisfy a specific sheaf condition (pullback preservation).

Equivalently, it is the category of nominal sets, which are sets equipped with an action by the group of permutations of a countable set of names 𝔸, such that every element has finite support.
