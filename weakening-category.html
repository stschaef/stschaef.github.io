Definition. Weakening a Category

2026-02-26 · displayed-category-theory

For categories 𝒞︀ and 𝒟︀, we can define the weakening of 𝒟︀ over 𝒞︀ as a displayed category over 𝒞︀ which trivially displays a copy of 𝒟︀ over each object of 𝒞︀.
