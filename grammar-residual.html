Definition. Grammar Residuals

2026-10-01 · parsing lambekd

For formal grammars 𝐴 and 𝐵, the residual 𝐴⊸𝐵 (sometimes called the lollipop or linear implication) is the right adjoint to the grammar tensor 𝐴⊗𝐵. It describes strings that, when prefixed by a string matching 𝐴, will match 𝐵.

Formally, it is defined as:

(𝐴⊸𝐵)(𝑣)=Π𝑢𝐴(𝑢)→𝐵(𝑢·𝑣)

Intuitively, a parse of 𝐴⊸𝐵 at a string 𝑣 is a function that takes any prefix string 𝑢 and a parse of 𝑢 in 𝐴, and produces a parse of the full concatenated string 𝑢·𝑣 in 𝐵.
