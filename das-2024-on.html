Reference. On the complexity of normalization for the planar 𝜆-calculus

Anupam Das, Damiano Mazza, Lê Thành Dũng Nguyên, Noam Zeilberger · arXiv · 2024 · complexity · arXiv
We sketch a tentative proof of P-completeness for the 𝛽-convertibility problem on untyped planar (a.k.a. ordered or non-commutative) 𝜆-terms.
